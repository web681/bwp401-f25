About Multaqa

Learning lives in every encounter, every experience, and every person we meet.

WHY THIS GUIDE?

We believe campus life
goes beyond lectures.

Multaqa brings events together in one place so students can discover new interests and join cultural, sports, arts, and technology activities.

This is a Web Programming 1 (BWP401) student project, not an official university platform. The content, dates, and venues are fictional examples demonstrating the guide.

A CLEAR SPACE FOR EVERYONE

How do we suggest and publish an event?

Suggesting an event

On the Contact page, select “Suggest an event” and include:

  • The event title, description, and intended audience.
  • The date, time, venue, or joining method.
  • The organizer’s name and any participation requirements.
Share your idea

Publishing guidelines

  • Provide clear, accurate information and respect image rights.
  • Use content suitable for the campus community that respects everyone.
  • Do not publish personal information without permission.
  • Review the details and update them when anything changes.

Illustrative policies: this form does not receive actual submissions.
